# Manufacturing Philosophy

NorthWood products are designed to be modular, durable, and easy to assemble.

The company prioritizes:

- High-quality engineered wood.
- Durable hardware components.
- Modern minimalist design.
- Easy replacement of damaged components.
- Efficient manufacturing processes.
