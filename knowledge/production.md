# Production

NorthWood Manufacturing produces modular furniture for modern offices and homes. Production processes are organized around five main product categories:

- Office Desk
- Storage Cabinet
- Bookshelf
- Office Chair
- Bedside Table

The manufacturing process emphasizes modular construction, durable materials, efficient assembly, surface finishing, quality control, and easy replacement of damaged components.

---

# Office Desk Production

Office desks are manufactured as modular work surfaces designed for home offices, corporate workspaces, and professional environments.

### Main Production Stages

1. **Material Preparation**
   - MDF boards and plywood are prepared according to the required dimensions.
   - Steel or aluminum structural components are prepared when required.
   - Edge banding and assembly hardware are prepared for the workstation.

2. **Panel Cutting and Preparation**
   - Wooden panels are cut into work surfaces, structural panels, supports, and other required components.
   - Surfaces are sanded and prepared for finishing.

3. **Structural Component Preparation**
   - Steel legs, steel frames, or aluminum frames are prepared depending on the desk design.
   - Components are checked for correct dimensions and structural integrity.

4. **Edge Finishing**
   - Exposed panel edges receive appropriate edge banding.
   - Edge banding may be white, black, or oak depending on the furniture finish.

5. **Surface Finishing**
   - Surfaces may receive primer, paint, wood stain, or varnish.
   - Fine sanding is performed before final finishing when required.

6. **Assembly**
   - Panels and structural components are assembled using wood screws, adhesives, and other required hardware.
   - Legs and structural supports are securely attached to the work surface.

7. **Quality Control**
   - The desk is inspected for structural stability.
   - Dimensions, surface quality, edge finishing, and assembly quality are verified.
   - The final product must be stable, functional, and free from visible manufacturing defects.

### Typical Materials

- MDF Board
- Plywood
- Steel Legs
- Steel Frame
- Aluminum Frame
- Wood Screws
- Wood Glue
- Edge Banding
- Primer
- Paint
- Wood Stain
- Varnish
- Sandpaper

# Storage Cabinet Production

Storage cabinets are manufactured as modular storage systems for office supplies, documents, personal belongings, and other items.

### Main Production Stages

1. **Material Preparation**
   - MDF boards and plywood are prepared for cabinet panels, shelves, doors, and structural components.
   - Hardware components are prepared according to the cabinet configuration.

2. **Panel Cutting and Preparation**
   - Panels are cut into cabinet sides, top and bottom panels, shelves, doors, and drawer components when required.
   - Surfaces are sanded and prepared for finishing.

3. **Cabinet Structure Assembly**
   - Main cabinet panels are assembled into the structural body.
   - Shelves and internal supports are installed.

4. **Door and Drawer Preparation**
   - Hinged doors use appropriate hinges.
   - Sliding doors use the required sliding mechanisms.
   - Drawer-based cabinets use drawer slides.
   - Handles are installed on doors and drawers.

5. **Edge Finishing**
   - Exposed panel edges receive suitable edge banding.
   - Edge finishing is selected according to the cabinet's appearance.

6. **Surface Finishing**
   - Cabinet components may receive primer, paint, wood stain, or varnish.
   - Surfaces are sanded between finishing stages when required.

7. **Final Assembly**
   - Doors, drawers, shelves, handles, and other hardware are installed.
   - Components are adjusted to ensure smooth operation.

8. **Quality Control**
   - Cabinet stability is inspected.
   - Doors and drawers are checked for correct alignment and movement.
   - Hardware installation and surface finishing are inspected.

### Typical Materials

- MDF Board
- Plywood
- Drawer Slides
- Soft-Close Hinges
- Metal Handles
- Shelf Supports
- Wood Screws
- Wood Glue
- Contact Adhesive
- Edge Banding
- Primer
- Paint
- Wood Stain
- Varnish
- Sandpaper

# Office Chair Production

Office chairs are manufactured as ergonomic seating systems designed for professional and home office environments.

### Main Production Stages

1. **Frame Preparation**
   - Steel frames and structural components are prepared.
   - Structural components are checked for strength and dimensional accuracy.

2. **Structural Assembly**
   - The main chair frame is assembled.
   - Structural components are secured before upholstery and finishing operations.

3. **Cushion Preparation**
   - Foam cushioning components are prepared according to the chair design.
   - Cushion components are positioned on the required seating and support areas.

4. **Upholstery**
   - Fabric upholstery is installed over the required padded components.
   - Upholstery is tensioned and secured to provide a clean and durable finish.

5. **Component Installation**
   - Chair mechanisms, armrests, headrests, and other components are installed when required.
   - Nylon casters and other mobility components are attached.

6. **Final Assembly**
   - The chair structure, seat, backrest, base, and supporting components are assembled.
   - Moving components are checked for correct operation.

7. **Quality Control**
   - Structural stability is inspected.
   - The chair is checked for proper movement and ergonomic functionality.
   - Upholstery and visible surfaces are inspected for defects.

### Typical Materials

- Steel Frame
- Foam Cushion
- Fabric Upholstery
- Plastic Components
- Nylon Casters
- Assembly Hardware

# General Production Workflow

NorthWood follows a standardized manufacturing workflow across its furniture categories.

### 1. Material Preparation

Required materials are selected and prepared according to the product category and manufacturing requirements.

### 2. Cutting and Component Preparation

Raw panels and structural components are transformed into the dimensions required by the furniture design.

### 3. Surface Preparation

Components are sanded and prepared for assembly or finishing.

### 4. Structural Assembly

Main furniture components are assembled using appropriate hardware, screws, and adhesives.

### 5. Hardware Installation

Functional components such as drawer slides, hinges, handles, shelf supports, legs, frames, and casters are installed according to the product category.

### 6. Surface Finishing

Furniture components may be painted, stained, varnished, veneered, or otherwise finished according to the intended design.

### 7. Final Assembly

Finished components are assembled into the final furniture product.

### 8. Quality Control

The final product is inspected for:

- Structural stability
- Correct dimensions
- Hardware functionality
- Surface quality
- Edge finishing
- Assembly quality
- Visible manufacturing defects

### 9. Ready for Inventory

Products that pass quality control are considered finished goods and can be transferred to inventory for sale or further distribution.
